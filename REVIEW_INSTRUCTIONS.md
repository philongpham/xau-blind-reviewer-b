# Independent visual review

Review these 30 charts independently. Do not access the other reviewer's package, the source candidate list, or the mapping key before submitting labels. The cards show XAUUSD Bid candles in UTC. Only completed candles at or before the decision cutoff are shown; the dashed vertical mark on M5 is the cutoff.

For each card, use the CSV template and record:

- `visual_pattern`: `clear`, `ambiguous`, or `rejected`. Judge whether a recognizable two-bottom or two-top reversal is visible from the plotted price action. A `clear` label needs two distinct test/rejection legs separated by a meaningful retracement and a visible reversal/break of the intervening reaction level by the cutoff. Use `ambiguous` when the view cannot support a stable call; use `rejected` when the shape is absent or only noise.
- `pattern_side`: `bottom`, `top`, `neither`, or `uncertain`.
- `two_distinct_rejections`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`.
- `meaningful_large_zone`: `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`, based only on the context visible in H4/H12/D1 at the cutoff.
- `trap_interpretation`: `plausible`, `insufficient_data`, or `no`. Treat a trapped-trader story as a hypothesis: OHLC charts cannot establish actual positions, stops, or order flow.
- `confidence_1to5` and `notes`: record uncertainty and missing context rather than forcing a label.

Do not use future price action, P&L, scanner output, or a desired trading result to decide labels. Preserve uncertain and conflicting labels.
